# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Founder-led B2B SaaS, tech-enabled services, and established services companies across North America, usually 10 to 200 employees and $1M to $50M in revenue. The primary buyer is a founder, CEO, COO, CRO, or customer success leader who has pressure to show AI progress, protect retention, or create pipeline without adding a large team.

## Product Purpose

Turn qualified visitors into 20-minute fit calls by proving that Sheila Kwan can translate AI pressure into working revenue systems. The site must also give not-yet-ready visitors a useful next step through the AI Reality Check. Success is measured by qualified fit calls booked, scorecard opt-ins, and clear trust built through real operator results.

## Brand Personality

Warm, precise, and candid. The voice is an experienced operator speaking plainly to a CEO over coffee: short sentences, uncomfortable truths stated kindly, numbers over adjectives, and zero AI hype.

## Anti-references

Generic AI consultancy sites, cream-and-serif consulting templates, breathless futurism, dark neon technology branding, fake social proof, vague capability claims, stock handshake imagery, decorative dashboards, and marketing language such as "cutting-edge," "revolutionize," "unlock," or "leverage synergies." The site must not feel like an agency selling a deck or a developer selling a chatbot.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the cost of the current problem, then show the shortest credible path to a working result.
2. Use receipts over adjectives: real revenue, retention, adoption, and delivery numbers carry the authority.
3. Make the commitment gradient obvious: free scorecard, honest fit call, fixed-fee audit, then implementation.
4. Qualify openly. Honest scarcity and a visible not-for-you list build more trust than manufactured urgency.
5. Show the operating method. Diagnose, Build, Adopt must feel concrete, repeatable, and owned by the client.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard access, focus visibility, semantics, form feedback, and reduced motion. The site must work at 360px without horizontal overflow, keep tap targets at least 44px, use plain language, and avoid color-only meaning.
